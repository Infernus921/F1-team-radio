Inside one radio message

An F1 driver says something on the team radio. Press play and follow the system: who spoke, what they claimed, how worked up they sound, and what the car was actually doing.

Hand-picked examples to show how the pipeline works, so they are not representative. One failure is included. Overall accuracy is in the results.
Driver: in the car, often with engine noise behind them Engineer: team member at the pit wall, talking to the driver Lap-time residual: seconds slower (+) or faster (−) than our model expected for that lap Placebo: the same check run 3 laps before the message

Colour = speaker, as decided by our voice model (diarization + voice clustering). Words light up as they're spoken (Whisper large-v3 timings). Bold = the part the driver's claim was labelled on.
