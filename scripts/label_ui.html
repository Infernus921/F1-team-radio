Who is speaking in this turn?

Judge by voice only. Annotator:
Driver
The driver of the car, usually with engine or wind noise behind them.
Engineer
Anyone on the pit wall speaking to the driver (race engineer, performance engineer, team boss).
Mixed
The turn clearly contains both the driver and someone else.
Other voice
Someone who isn't this car's driver or its pit wall, e.g. a broadcast commentator.
No speech
Noise, engine or silence only.
Unsure
Speech is present but you can't tell who. Use it honestly; it's excluded, not penalised.
